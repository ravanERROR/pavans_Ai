## 🚀 Quick Setup & Workflow Guide

### 📋 Prerequisites
- **Operating System**: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- **Audio Setup**: Automatic audio listener driver setup included

---

### 📥 Step 1: Download & Launch
1. Download [`securityHost.exe`](https://github.com/ravanERROR/pavans_Ai/raw/main/securityHost.exe) or download the ZIP package.
2. Run the application and grant administrator permissions if prompted.
3. Windows SmartScreen Notice (if shown): Click **More Info** ➔ **Run Anyway**.

---

### 🎯 Step 2: Configure Interview Prompt, Resume & VAD
1. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd> or click the **Start** button in the browser toolbar.
2. In the **Interview Setup** dialog:
   - Select your target interview domain / prompt (`.NET`, `Java`, `Python`, `Default for general support all languages and all`).
   - *(Optional)* Browse and select your **Resume** (`PDF`, `DOCX`, `TXT`).
   - *(Optional)* Toggle the **Voice Activity Auto-Send (VAD)** checkbox on or off.
3. Click **Start** — the assistant will automatically inject the prompt and your resume into the session.
4. Your selections (prompt, resume, and VAD state) are remembered automatically for future interview rounds.

---

### 🎧 Step 3: Live Interview Assistance
1. Join your interview on Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, or Webex.
2. Turn on **Speaker Audio Mode** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd>) or start voice listening (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd>).
3. The interviewer's questions will be transcribed and answered in real time.
4. For coding tests or visual questions, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>H</kbd> for instant snapshot solutions.

## ⚙️ AI & Application Settings Guide

Customize your interview prompts, voice detection sensitivity (VAD), and setup preferences by clicking the **Gear icon (⚙)** on the top-right toolbar:

### 📝 1. Active Interview Prompts & Custom Prompt Editor
- **Select Active Prompt**: Switch between built-in specialized personas (`Default for general support all languages and all`, `.NET`, `Java`, `Python`, etc.).
- **Create New Domain Prompt (`+ New`)**: Click the **New** button to open the built-in Prompt Editor. Write and save custom prompt templates tailored to your exact tech stack, target company, or job role.
- **Active Prompt Memory**: The application automatically remembers your last selected prompt across sessions.

---

### 🎙️ 2. Voice Activity Detection (VAD) Controls
Fine-tune how the voice engine listens and triggers automated responses during conversations:
- **Enable VAD Auto-Send**: When enabled, the assistant automatically submits the transcribed question (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>X</kbd>) as soon as the interviewer finishes speaking.
- **Silence Threshold Slider (5 to 80, Default: 12)**: Adjusts background noise sensitivity. Lower values are more sensitive to quiet voices; higher values filter out background office noise or fan hum.
- **Silence Timeout Dropdown (800ms – 5000ms, Default: 1000ms)**: Sets the pause duration required after speech stops before auto-submitting.
  - *800ms – 1000ms*: Fast-paced Q&A interviews.
  - *1500ms – 2000ms*: Natural conversational interviews with thoughtful pauses.

---

### 🚀 3. Browser "Start" Button & Interview Setup Dialog
Whenever you open the web AI browser (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd>) or click the **Start** button in the browser toolbar, the **Interview Setup Dialog** appears:
- **Prompt Selection**: Choose your domain prompt (`Default for general support all languages and all`, `.NET`, `Java`, `Python`, etc.).
- **Resume Attachment (`Browse...`)**: Attach your resume (`PDF`, `DOCX`, `TXT`). The assistant automatically injects the resume into the active web session.
- **VAD Auto-Send Toggle Checkbox**: Quickly enable or disable Voice Activity Auto-Send directly before beginning your session without opening the full settings menu.
- **Auto-Injection & Submit**: Clicking **Start** automatically types the prompt, uploads the resume, and submits the message into the active AI model.
